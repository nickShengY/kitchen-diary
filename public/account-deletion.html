Kitchen Diary

Request account and data deletion

Kitchen Diary is operated by Robopioneer Inc. You can request deletion without signing in to the app.

In the native iPhone/iPad app, open Diary → Settings → Delete account. Confirm and sign in again. This deletes the Firebase account, nested cloud data (including nutrition, pantry, drafts and history), public profile, owned recipes and community records. The service must be available for deletion to complete; if it fails, retry rather than assuming deletion succeeded.

  1. Email robopioneer.product@gmail.com with the subject “Kitchen Diary account deletion”.
  2. Include the Google or Apple account email connected to Kitchen Diary. State whether you want your entire account removed or only specified data, such as nutrition logs. Do not send your password or payment-card details.
  3. Support may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request and will confirm the scope and any records that must be retained.

Data covered by your request

You may request removal of your account and public profile, authored recipes, and account-linked saved data, including pantry state, meal plans, nutrition goals and logs, water intake, cooking history, collections, and achievements. Identify community posts you also want removed.

Older app versions may only remove some records. The native Apple deletion flow removes nested cloud records too; contact support if you previously deleted your account in an older version.

Local copies, billing, and retention

Clearing device storage removes only that device's local copy; signing out does not delete cloud data. Other devices may retain local copies until you clear them there.

Apple, Google Play and any historical payment provider may retain purchase, billing, or legal records under their own policies. Account deletion does not cancel subscriptions; manage Apple subscriptions in your Apple account and Google Play subscriptions in Google Play. Purchase ownership/audit records and a deletion marker may remain to prevent purchase fraud and deleted data being recreated.

Synced account data has no configured automatic expiry. We have not published a fixed processing deadline or additional retention period for deletion requests. Support will explain any applicable retention requirement when handling your request.
